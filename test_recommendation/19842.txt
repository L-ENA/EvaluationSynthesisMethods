## **Recommendations:** 

The recommendations have been formulated based on the findings which were analyzed through primary and secondary data. The recommendations are divided between those which are common to all the cells across all 3 categories and those which are specific to each category, and are formulated specific to the two main stakeholders, i.e., the government and UNICEF. Broadly, the recommendations are focused on addressing the need for establishment of district-level cells in all the six states, continued need of technical assistance and aid in handover process from UNICEF, capacity building and skill development of health officials at the state, district and block level, and the need for increased agency for cells to lead SBCC programming and have a greater say in budget and timeline planning. We also recommend, a similar evaluation to assess the progress of the transition of the state and decide future course of action at the end of the next programmatic cycle of five years.

## 8. Policy implications and recommendations 

The objective of this evaluation exercise was to understand the SBCC Cells’ performance towards maximizing utilization of allocated resources and augmenting the results of the health systems strengthening initiatives and other cross sectoral themes of social and health determinants at the state and district level. A qualitative research design was adopted for the same. Findings from this study have provided insights into the status of the existing SBCC cells and identified certain areas for improvement in its functioning. 

The suggestive recommendations for strengthening the functioning of SBCC cells are **based on the findings from study, field observations, consultations with stakeholders.** 

Confidence in the generalizability of the findings and recommendations is enhanced by the comprehensive strategy and design used for the **selection of the states and arena of stakeholders interviewed as a part of this evaluation** .
